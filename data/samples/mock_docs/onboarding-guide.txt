Customer Onboarding Guide (Internal, Demo Content)

New accounts are assigned an owner from the customer success team within
two business days of the contract being signed. The owner is responsible
for a kickoff call, a 30-day check-in, and a 90-day account review.

Riverton Robotics was onboarded by Dana Kim in January and is currently in
its 90-day review window. Blue Harbor Logistics was onboarded by Marcus Lee
and requested a dedicated integration engineer due to the size of their
shipment data feed.

Accounts marked "At Risk" in the CRM should be escalated to the account
owner's manager if there has been no successful contact within 14 days.
Fenwick & Cole Legal was marked At Risk after two missed check-in calls.
