Data Handling Policy Summary (Internal, Demo Content)

All customer account data is scoped to the owning tenant and is never
shared across tenants. Account owners may only view accounts assigned to
them or accounts belonging to their direct reports.

Northgate Pharma is subject to additional handling requirements due to
its industry, and all account notes must be reviewed by the compliance
team before being shared externally. Solstice Retail Group has no special
handling requirements beyond the standard policy.
